# Neobrutalism Design Guidelines (@reactbits-pro/skill-neobrutalism)

### Core Rules:
1. **Borders**: 2.5px to 3px solid black borders on all cards, buttons, badges, and modals (`border-2 border-black` / `border-[2.5px] border-black`).
2. **Hard Shadows**: Zero-blur hard offset shadows (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #000`).
3. **Saturated Pop Colors**:
   - Electric Yellow: `#FFE600`
   - Electric Pink / Crimson: `#FF5B89` / `#FF4343`
   - Electric Lime / Green: `#4EFE82`
   - Electric Cyan / Blue: `#00E5FF`
   - Vibrant Violet: `#A259FF`
   - High contrast white `#FFFFFF` & black `#000000`
4. **Tactile Physics on Interactive Elements**:
   - `active:translate-x-[2px] active:translate-y-[2px] active:shadow-[1px_1px_0px_0px_#000]`
5. **Chunky Bold Typography**:
   - Heavy weights (`font-black`, `uppercase`, `tracking-wider`), punchy high-contrast badges.
